About Me

A little bit about who I am

I'm a 22-year-old IT graduate with a love for structured data and creative storytelling. I graduated with a degree in Information Technology from Batangas State University, where I served as a student representative and honed my skills in business analytics.

Interestingly, my first passion wasn't tech—it was Culinary Arts. While I chose the IT path, I still bring that same "chef's precision" to my technical work. I'm a firm believer that whether you're crafting a line of code or a meal, the details matter most.

Currently based in Makati, I spend my free time reading and watching fantasy and BL genre materials, listening to podcasts, or cooking.

My Projects

Some of my recent work

Inventory System Multi-Channel

Multi-Channel Inventory Management System

An inventory management system for BMSuit that synchronizes stock levels across Shopee, TikTok Shop, and physical storefronts. The system features a real-time analytics dashboard for tracking key metrics and a dedicated inventory module to manage multi-channel product movement. By consolidating these platforms into a single interface, the project streamlines operations and ensures data accuracy across all sales channels.

QR-Based University

ACIS: ACTION Center Inventory System

An inventory system for the BatStateU-ACTION Center to streamline the management of critical office supplies, emergency kits, and rescue equipment. The platform features a real-time dashboard for high-level asset tracking and inventory module for efficient stock handling. To modernize the lending process, we integrated a QR-based borrowing system, allowing the department to easily manage and track equipment loans to other university offices with increased speed and accuracy.
